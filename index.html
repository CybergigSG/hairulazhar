02Why I built KatalockAi: one person shouldn't have to be ten
I built Katalock because I was tired of watching great businesses lose to bad tools.

Across India and Southeast Asia I kept meeting the same person: a founder, a shop owner, a freelancer, up past midnight doing ten jobs at once. Research, design, copy, code, GST, ads, support. Alone.

Western AI tools gave them answers, but an answer isn't a team. Those tools didn't speak Bahasa, Tamil or Mandarin, didn't understand GST or UPI, and were priced for San Francisco, not a small business in Jaipur or Bengaluru. So I built a matrix of specialist agents that actually do the work, with a small team in Singapore and India, with no VC pressure and no bloat. We started with the API, then built the workspace. Today it runs from ₹299 a month up to done-for-you agent teams.

05K-Computer: why I built it, and where it's going

Most AI products send every question to one expensive model and hope it's right. I couldn't afford that, and I didn't trust it either. So I built around it: K-Computer is the orchestration layer under Katalock Workspace, Code and Studio.

Live today

A panel, not a single model

K-Computer asks several independent models from different labs the same question, checks the sources they cite, and has separate judges reconcile their answers.

  • One answer with a score showing how strongly the models agreed, and a clear note wherever they didn't.
  • Runs automatically for questions about law, money, health and the news.
  • In Code, it reviews the apps people build before they ship them.

The next frontier · in development

The K-Computer pyramid

A four-tier Mixture-of-Agents pyramid of specialist panels. A question starts at the base and climbs only as far as it needs to.

AiM · Apex boardConvenes every panel; called only when the tiers below deadlock
Stargazer · Deep reasoningComplex code and multi-step workflows↑
Tara · Regional logicVernacular and local business rules for India, Indonesia and Singapore↑
Lucy · Fast routingRouting, structuring and everyday work, quickly and at the lowest cost↑
♕Every tier has its own arbiter, the Queen, whose job is to force a consensus before anything moves up. Escalation only happens when consensus fails.
~85% of tasks resolved at the base ~10x lower running cost than sending everything to the top Distillation flywheel: apex answers train the smaller models
The pyramid is in development. Figures are design targets, not measured results.
Why it matters

Models are rented and replaceable. The edge is the orchestration that decides which models to ask, how to weigh them and how to check them. Next is the flywheel: every hard problem the apex solves becomes training data for smaller models, so the base gets smarter and the pyramid gets cheaper over time. And a dedicated regional tier covers the vernacular and local business logic the big labs overlook.

06Research: The Harness Is the System

Deployed capability of a large language model is a property of a system, not of weights alone. My open research paper treats everything built around a pretrained model, the harness, as a first-class object of study: a Rust “nervous system” for tokenizers, data loading, serving, orchestration, gateway and agent control, and a reinforcement learning loop centred on GRPO whose heavy kernels belong in C and C++.

Open community research paper · v1 · 20 November 2026 · 34 pages

The Harness Is the System: A Rust Nervous System and a C/C++ Reinforcement Learning Loop Around a Pretrained Brain

Central research questionCan one verifiable contract across inference, trajectory provenance, reward verification and policy optimisation measurably reduce training/inference mismatch, improve reproducibility, and make long-horizon agents more reliable, for a fixed pretrained model?
  • Brain and harness, separated, with reliability, provenance and verifiability as the thread through every component.
  • One trajectory contract, a fixed-layout C ABI shared by serving, provenance, reward checking and training.
  • GRPO from first principles, through its 2025 and 2026 variants, verifiable rewards and training/inference mismatch.
  • A discipline of falsification: a minimal build target with acceptance criteria stated before any results, and a failure boundary for every recommendation.
RustTokenizationPagedAttentionFlashAttentionKV cacheGRPO / DAPO / GSPORLVRC ABISYCL & WebGPU

Open draft for review, not peer reviewed. It specifies an architecture and a measurement plan; no experimental results are claimed yet. 57 numbered references.

How I engineered it
⚡Live, interruptible calls

The call's audio streams to the build server, which detects when the caller stops, transcribes, and speaks the reply sentence by sentence. If the caller talks over it, the agent stops at once and listens.

🌐The website as memory

It reads up to 40 pages, refreshes daily and only re-indexes pages that changed, then writes an editable fact sheet. When the answer isn't there, the agent says it isn't sure instead of guessing a price.

📅Bookings that don't clash

It checks free times, offers a few, reads the details back and books only after a clear yes. It never double-books.

📞Checks with the team

It can ring people only from an approved list, ask the question while the caller holds, and relay the answer. No answer in about 80 seconds and it offers to take a message.

🤝Warm transfer

The human hears a short briefing on who is calling and why, presses 1 to take it, and the rest of the call is transcribed into the log.

$Metered and capped

Credits are checked before a call starts, billed by the second at 2.5 credits a minute (about S$0.07) when it ends, with a 20-minute cap. Each line has its own secret webhook key.

08Katalock for Chrome: it works in your own tabs while you watch

A Chrome extension that brings Katalock into the tabs you share. It reads the page, moves a visible cursor, clicks and types while you watch, checks its work with a second model, and asks before anything is sent, booked or paid. Move your mouse and it stops at once.

Katalock types in your tab while you watch. The send waits for approval, with a second model's check and its confidence.
Move your mouse and Katalock stops at once. Hand back and it carries on without typing anything twice.
WhatsApp Web: it replies by rules you agree for each contact, and holds the rest for you.
Google Calendar: clashes and time zones are shown before you approve a booking.
🛡Nothing runs that wasn't shipped

The server sends actions as data, like “click e12”; the extension's own bundled code performs them. No code is fetched or evaluated.

✋Approval on both sides

Sends, submits, bookings, payments and deletes are caught by the planner's flag and by the button's own label, and always stop for your yes.

🔒Private by default

Access to katalock.net only; every other site is granted when you share that tab. Passwords, card numbers and one-time codes are masked before anything leaves the page.

11Katalock for Education: IBTISAM teaches, students do the thinking

AI can do homework in seconds. That's the problem: students paste the question, copy the answer and learn nothing, and teachers can't tell who really understands. So I built IBTISAM, an AI tutor for schools that explains, draws, quizzes and coaches, and never does the homework for them. Teachers see how well every student understands each topic.

IBTISAM · launch video
IInteractiveBBasedTTutoringIIntelligence forSStudyAAndMMastery

Ibtisam also means “a smile”. Learning should feel like one.

katalock.net/edu
1Teachers make a class

Subject, topic, what students should learn, and the students' emails. Like a project folder for the class.

2Students learn with IBTISAM

They chat about the topic with diagrams, drawings, slides and quick quizzes, in their own language.

3Teachers see understanding

Every quiz answer counts towards a learning objective, so each student gets a real percentage with the answers behind it.

Asked to just write the answer, IBTISAM asks for the student's own ideas, draws the leaf, then checks understanding with a quiz.
Sketch on a photo: students snap a worksheet and IBTISAM circles and labels where to look, without filling in the answers.
IBTISAM drafts the marking against the teacher's rubric, quoting the student's own words. The teacher checks it and decides.
The teacher's view: mastery per student and per objective, and who needs help.
How I engineered the guardrails
⚖Help levels, set per class

Four allowed levels, from explaining a concept to helping with an outline. Level five, doing the work, is always refused, and the teacher sets the ceiling.

🛡Can't be talked out of it

Urgency, role-play, “my teacher allows it”, or asking for the essay one paragraph at a time all get the same kind boundary and a next learning step.

✓Quizzes that measure

IBTISAM ends ideas with a multiple-choice check tied to a named objective. The app marks it and rolls it into the student's mastery score.

⏱Homework, quiz, exam, project

Homework gets hints only. Exams are timed with IBTISAM switched off, copying blocked and leaving the page noted. Projects are coached against a rubric.

🔒Built for primary schools

Only verified teachers with a school or Ministry of Education email. Students see only their own classes and are told their teacher can read the chats.

🌏In the student's language

English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Bahasa Indonesia, Javanese, Sundanese, Madurese, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese, with notes, slides and sheets as Word, PowerPoint, Excel or PDF.
